title=Map Teleport – Vehicle & Trailer Add-on
description=WORLD-MAP TELEPORT
description=For B42.21 single-player and multiplayer. Adds independent teleport options to the world-map right-click menu.
description=
description=MOD MENU COEXISTENCE
description=Map, navigation/GPS, route, destination, marker, teleport and cheat-menu options from other mods can appear together and remain separately selectable.
description=
description=CHARACTERS & ANIMALS
description=Teleport Here: Teleports your character and carried animals. Also allows character-only teleport while seated. When on foot, rope-led animals travel with you and are reattached on arrival.
description=
description=VEHICLES & TRAILERS
description=Teleport Vehicle Here: Use while seated to teleport your character, passengers, vehicle and entire connected towing chain, including trailers, animal trailers and animals inside the vehicles.
description=Stop the convoy before teleporting. Your character and passengers arrive together at the teleport point, outside the vehicles. Vehicles are placed on nearby open ground large enough for the convoy, including roads, parking areas, unobstructed grass, dirt and similar terrain.
description=
description=TEMPORARY VEHICLE MARKER
description=After a vehicle teleport, a steering-wheel marker shows the vehicle's landing position on the map. The next vehicle teleport replaces the marker, preventing multiple markers from accumulating. In multiplayer, the marker appears on the map of the player who initiated the teleport.
description=
description=VEHICLE MOD COMPATIBILITY
description=Supports vanilla vehicles and modded vehicles using the game's native vehicle, towing and vehicle-animal systems, including standard KI5 vehicles, Autotsar Trailers B42, W900 Semi-Truck B42, Carry More Animal and others.
description=
description=BACKUP KEYBOARD SHORTCUT
description=Bind UMTSP: Teleport Here (World Map) under Options → Key Bindings → Universal Map Teleport. Open the map, point at your destination and press the key. While seated, choose character or vehicle teleport. Unbound by default.
description=
description=REQUIREMENTS
description=Requires Map Teleport – Right-Click Rescue and ZombieBuddy 2.3.4+. Steam prompts you to subscribe to both. Multiplayer servers and clients must have the same mods and matching versions.
description=
description=【地图右键传送】
description=面向 B42.21 单人及多人游戏，在世界地图右键菜单加入独立的传送选项。
description=
description=【模组菜单共存】
description=其他地图、导航/GPS、路线、目的地、标记、传送及作弊菜单等选项可以同时显示、分别选择。
description=
description=【人物与动物】
description=Teleport Here：传送人物及携带动物，坐车时也可单独传人。步行时可带绳牵动物一起传送，到达后恢复牵引。
description=
description=【车辆与拖车】
description=Teleport Vehicle Here：坐车时使用，将人物、乘客、车辆及整条牵引车队一起传送，包括拖车、动物拖车和车内动物。
description=车队停稳后即可传送。人物与乘客一并到达传送点并处于下车状态，车辆停放在附近可容纳车队的空地，包括道路、停车场及无障碍草地、泥地等地形。
description=
description=【车辆临时标记】
description=传车后，地图显示车辆落点的方向盘标记；下次传车时标记会刷新，不会在地图上留下多个标记。多人模式下，标记显示在发起传送的玩家地图上。
description=
description=【车辆模组兼容】
description=支持原版及使用游戏原生车辆、牵引和车内动物机制的模组车辆，包括 KI5 普通车辆、Autotsar Trailers B42、W900 Semi-Truck B42、Carry More Animal 等。
description=
description=【备用键盘快捷键】
description=在 Options → Key Bindings → Universal Map Teleport 中绑定 UMTSP: Teleport Here (World Map)。打开地图，鼠标指向目的地后按键；坐车时可选择传人或传车。默认未绑定。
description=
description=【依赖】
